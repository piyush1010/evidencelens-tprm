ACME CLOUD SECURITY POLICY — DEMO DOCUMENT

Access control
ACME Cloud uses role-based access control and least-privilege access. Multi-factor
authentication is enforced across all corporate and production systems. Access rights
are reviewed quarterly by the Security team and removed within 24 hours when employment ends.

Encryption
Customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using
AES-256. Encryption keys are managed through a centralized key-management service
and rotated annually.

Incident response
ACME maintains a documented incident-response plan. Security incidents are triaged
by an on-call team. Customers are notified without undue delay after a confirmed
incident affecting their data. Tabletop exercises are conducted once per year.

Business continuity
Production data is backed up daily. Backups are encrypted and retained for 35 days.
Disaster-recovery exercises are performed annually. The stated recovery-time
objective is four hours and the recovery-point objective is 24 hours.

Vendor management
Critical subprocessors receive a security review before onboarding. Reviews include
security documentation and contractual data-protection obligations. This policy does
not state how often existing subprocessors are reassessed.

Vulnerability management
External penetration tests are performed annually. Critical vulnerabilities are
targeted for remediation within 15 days and high-severity vulnerabilities within
30 days.
