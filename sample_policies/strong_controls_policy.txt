SUMMIT SOFTWARE SECURITY POLICY — SYNTHETIC DEMO

Access control
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enforced across all corporate systems and all production
systems. Access is based on least privilege and reviewed quarterly.

Encryption
Customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256.
Encryption keys are centrally managed and rotated annually.

Incident response
Summit Software guarantees notification to customers within 24 hours after confirming a security
incident that affects customer data. The incident-response plan is tested annually.

Vendor management
Subprocessors undergo annual security assessments and contractual DPDP and GDPR compliance
reviews. Findings are tracked to remediation before renewal.
